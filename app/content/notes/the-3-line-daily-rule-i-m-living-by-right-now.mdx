I wasn’t failing because I lacked discipline.  
I was failing because I made my life **TOO COMPLICATED**.

Too many SYSTEMS.  
Too many PLANS.  
Too many RULES.

Every time I felt OVERWHELMED, I tried to fix it by adding more structure.  
And every time, I COLLAPSED under it.

So I did the opposite.

I removed almost everything.

What I’m left with now is just **THREE QUESTIONS I ANSWER EVERY DAY**.

### The 3 Questions (That’s It)

Every day, I only answer **YES OR NO** to these:

 **MOVE**  
Did I move my body today?  
A jog. A walk. Pushups. Anything intentional.

Not fitness goals.  
Not aesthetics.  
Just movement.

 **BUILD**  
Did I spend 2 focused hours building ONE thing?  
One Framer template.  
One landing page.  
Not five ideas. Not multitasking.

One thing.

 **SHIP**  
Did I produce proof of work?  
A screenshot.  
A clip.  
A commit.  
An export.

UGLY is fine. INCOMPLETE is fine.  
HIDDEN progress doesn’t count.

### Why This Works (When Everything Else Failed)

This rule doesn’t ask how I feel.  
It doesn’t care about motivation.  
It doesn’t reward perfection.

It only checks **ACTION**.

If I hit **2 OUT OF 3** , the day counts.  
If I hit **1 OR 0** , I don’t punish myself.

I reset tomorrow.

No guilt spiral.  
No dramatic restart.  
No “I’ve failed my life” speeches.

Just reset.

### The Tracking Method (Even Simpler)

I don’t use NOTION.  
I don’t use APPS.  
I don’t use DASHBOARD.

I use **ONE NOTE**.

That’s it.
    
    
    DATE:
    
    MOVE:   YES / NO
    BUILD:  YES / NO
    SHIP:   YES / NO
    
    ONE LINE NOTE:
    

No journaling.  
No reflections.  
No emotions.

Just facts.

### What This Taught Me

I didn’t need more discipline.  
I needed **FEWER DECISIONS**.

I didn’t need motivation.  
I needed **A FLOOR I COULDN’T FALL BELOW**.

Some days are BAD.  
Some days are empty.  
Some days I BARELY show up.

But this system survives bad days.

And that’s the point.

### If You’re Tired of Starting Over

STOP asking yourself big questions like:

  * “What’s my PURPOSE?”

  * “What should I be DOING with my life?”

  * “Why am I not CONSISTENT?”




Ask smaller ones:

Did I MOVE?  
Did I BUILD?  
Did I SHIP?

That’s enough for today.
